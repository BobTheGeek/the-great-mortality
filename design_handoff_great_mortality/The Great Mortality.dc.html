  • Card flip: tap anywhere on the card; back flips too.
  • Codex term: tap → popover anchored to the word; scrim tap closes.
  • No hover, no long-press menus, no gestures beyond tap and scroll.

A CHRONICLE · 1348
Fear and Blame
When the sickness came and no one could say why, people looked for someone to blame. In towns across Europe, frightened neighbors accused Jewish families of poisoning the wells. It was false. Jewish families drank from the same wells and died of the same sickness. But fear does not wait for proof.
In many places the accusations led to terrible violence. In some, leaders refused to allow it. In 1348 Pope Clement VI declared the accusations false and ordered that Jewish people be protected, pointing out that the plague struck places with no Jewish community at all.
In your region, the steward stood at the gate. The record says simply: no one was harmed.
REMEMBER THIS
· A scapegoat is someone blamed for a thing they did not do.
· Fear plus ignorance is dangerous. Evidence is the cure for both.
· Some leaders protected people. Leadership is a choice.

Shell. Every screen is a two-leaf ledger inside a walnut frame: the frame absorbs the iPad safe areas and home indicator, the leaves hold content. Reference screens (Evidence, Codex, Chronicles, Cards) share one tab row along the top edge of the spread, with a sealed sixth tab reserved for Act II. Modals are loose sheets laid across the spine. The Mortality skin is parchment + walnut ink + seal red + gold leaf; the neutral shell skin at the bottom of this turn shows the same parts in plain linen.

Flow. Opening → name → map. Tap a town → town leaf replaces the ledger. End month → month report sheet → advisor (sometimes) → map. Tabs open reference screens; Lens stays sealed until all four questions are solved.

Through the Lens · August 1348
What was really happening
Rat colonies
Black rats lived in granaries, ships and thatch. The sickness killed them first. Yersinia pestis lived in their blood.
Flea spread
When the rats died, their fleas jumped to people, and rode in cloth and grain along every road and sea lane.
⌒⌒
Two forms
Bubonic, from flea bites, in the warm months. Pneumonic, a coughing form passed breath to breath, which is why winter slowed it in some towns and not in others.
The village with no road
Fontenera was spared not by its air or its prayers, but because no cart, cloth or rat ever climbed the footpath.

CODEX · NOUN · FROM THE ITALIAN QUARANTA, FORTY
Quarantine
WHAT IT MEANS HERE
Keeping ships, goods or people apart from everyone else for a set time, to see whether sickness shows itself before they come in.
WHY IT MATTERS
Your "hold ships offshore" decree is this idea, thirty years before anyone wrote it into law.
THEN · 1377
Ragusa held arrivals thirty days on an island; Venice later made it forty.
NOW
Still used. The length is set by how long a disease takes to show, not by the number forty.

1Main map — three layout directions (landscape 1180×820)

Direction. Parchment ground (#ece0c4 → #d9c69b vignette), walnut ink #4a2f1d, seal red #8b2e2e, gold leaf #b8902e used only for the illuminated moments. Type: Iowan Old Style for everything you read, Avenir for every number. Sea is hatched ink, roads are double lines, the footpath is dotted.

Severity without color. Each town carries three signals at once: a ring (none → dotted → dashed → solid heavy), an ink wash that spreads outward, and a one-word label beneath the name (Healthy, Rumors, Spreading, Ravaged, Burned out). Region-wide, the map darkens from the sea inward as deaths rise.

The three options differ in where the decrees live: 1a a hand of cards along the bottom, map full-bleed. 1b a bound ledger page on the right, map as the left leaf. 1c a sealed-scroll rail on the left, with the lamplit late-game state shown so you can see the dread building. Assumption: the HUD stays one row across the top in all three; End month is always the largest single target.
