## Content and simulation

- History and balance targets after the design handoff's `ADDENDUM.md` and Ole J. Benedictow,
  *The Black Death 1346–1353*; Boccaccio's *Decameron* for the "Fled" ending.
- The plague's true answer (1894) came from Alexandre Yersin and Paul-Louis Simond.
- Historical survival benchmark: roughly 60% survived (about 4 in 10 died across Europe).

## Art

- Map, town emblems, icons and the wax seal: the project's design handoff (`design_handoff_great_mortality/assets/`).
- Advisor portraits, myth-card woodcuts beyond the engravings above, and opening galley art
  remain placeholders from the design handoff.
